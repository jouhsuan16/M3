About

Why we formed, what we value, what we are aiming toward, and who is shaping the collective.

Why We Formed

Making space for divergence

M³ began as a thesis collective shaped by a shared refusal of singular paths. We came together because each of us was already testing different ways of making, thinking, and staying in relation without flattening difference.

Values

What we protect

We value disagreement, unfinished thinking, reciprocity, and attention to pace. We treat tension as something to work with rather than something that must always be resolved, and we resist turning collaboration into a performance of smooth agreement.

Goals

What we are building toward

Our goal is to build a collective practice that stays intellectually rigorous, emotionally sustainable, and publicly legible without becoming fixed. We use collaboration to widen what each project can become.

Collective Reflections

A short reflection on how consensus, disagreement, and experimentation shaped our collaboration in practice.

In Practice

Working through difference

Our collaboration began through slow text-based discussion and gradually shifted into more direct face-to-face exchange. Over time, we learned that consensus does not need to mean sameness, and that disagreement can remain part of the process rather than something to erase.

Instead of forcing early alignment, we give members room to test ideas independently, compare outcomes, and return to discussion with clearer positions. Conflict is treated as a productive resource, and minority views are kept visible rather than folded away.
